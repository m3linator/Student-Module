JSX::Element UpdatePrompt ( )

Registers the service worker and shows a banner once a new app version is available.

Installed web apps on iOS rarely re-check for updates on their own, so the worker is polled hourly and every time the app comes back to the foreground.

Rückgabe
The update banner, or nothing while the app is up to date.

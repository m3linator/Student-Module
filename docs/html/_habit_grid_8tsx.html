JSX::Element HabitGrid ( HabitGridProps props)

Compact Anki-style month heatmap: one small rounded square per day, Monday first.

Checked days are green, today is outlined, future days are pale and disabled. The visible square is smaller than its button, so the touch target stays 40 px on phones. The exact date is available as tooltip and accessible name; weekdays are single letters.
